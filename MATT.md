# Matt: how to get your stuff into megaZINE

## Once

1. Sam adds you as a collaborator on `samuelling/megazine` (GitHub → repo Settings → Collaborators). Accept the email invite.
2. Make sure your Claude can reach your GitHub (in the Claude app, GitHub under Settings → Connectors, or use Claude Code on your Mac).

## Every time

Paste this to Claude, then tell it what you want to add:

> I'm Matt Crump, co-editor of megaZINE with Sam Ling. The repo is github.com/samuelling/megazine. Please add it to this session, read CLAUDE.md in it, and follow it. I want to add some of my work.

Then just talk normally, e.g.:

- "Add my painting *Name*. Here's the photo. Made by hand."
- "Add my iOS app *Name*, TestFlight link is …, made with Claude."
- "Put my two newest pieces in Issue 02."

Things Claude will ask you if you don't say: how it was made (by hand, by Claude, or both) and where it lives (a link, or a photo for paintings).

## What happens to your entries

- Each one gets a gold **megaZINE seal** reading "MADE BY MATT" plus how it was made.
- Paintings show their photo on the site.
- If it goes in the current issue, Claude regenerates the printable sheet too.
